This guide covers alert status, acknowledgement, silencing, closing, and notification policies.

## At a glance

- Filter the list by status, time, cluster, or source.
- Acknowledged means someone is handling an alert; it does not mean the condition has recovered.
- Notification channels can be configured in the policy area.
